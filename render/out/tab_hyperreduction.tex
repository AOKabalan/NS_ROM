Median iterations and solve time are over each arm's \emph{converged} points only -- a diverged solve has no time to quote -- so the DEIM medians are taken over its converged subset, not the full set. The $\mu=$ NaN count is the diverged points plus a smaller number of converged points whose reduced eigenproblem has no real leading eigenvalue (a complex pair on the asymmetric branch); it therefore exceeds the divergence count rather than matching it.
